## Competitive tendency captured in Figma

- Wanderlog: itinerary-heavy / itinerary-first
- TripIt: booking/document-heavy / post-booking organizer
- PackPoint: packing-heavy
- Polarsteps: emotion/tracking-heavy
- Stippl: broad all-in-one, with risk of losing a clear product focus

## Check2GO opportunity identified in the analysis

The strongest differentiated direction is:

**checklist-first travel app + shared planning/preparation + future booking/payment in one calm interface**

Positioning lines captured from the analysis:
- “The clearest and calmest way to prepare a trip step by step.”
- “A checklist-first travel app that helps people prepare for a trip without chaos.”

The analysis argues this is more focused and differentiated than presenting Check2GO simply as another generic “all-in-one travel app”.

## Usability criteria used in the competitor analysis

1. Clarity of the core scenario
2. Ease of the first useful action
3. Information structure / trip hub
4. Trip-preparation support: checklists, documents, reminders, packing
5. Collaboration and scalability: sharing, collaboration, booking-readiness

## Product risks identified

- scope becoming too broad too early;
- weak checklist UX undermining the core differentiator;
- booking/payment introducing a much higher-complexity commerce layer.

## Recommended staged development

### Stage 1 — Win the wedge
Focus on:
- smart checklists;
- packing;
- documents;
- reminders;
- shared preparation.

### Stage 2 — Build the trip hub
Add:
- itinerary;
- routes;
- places;
- events;
- shared trip view.

### Stage 3 — Transaction-adjacent
Add:
- reservation import;
- confirmation storage;
- travel-document organization;
- price tracking;
- booking redirects.

Only after those stages should direct booking and payment become a major implementation priority.

## Viability conclusion

The viability analysis says the product is viable if it enters the market through the focused preparation/checklist problem rather than trying to launch immediately as a full travel super-app.
